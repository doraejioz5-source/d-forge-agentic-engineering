# Team prompt rules

1. Do not change schema field names without team agreement.
2. Do not invent numeric performance targets.
3. Every design module must point to REQ IDs.
4. Every test must point to REQ IDs.
5. Prefer concise engineering language over long prose.
6. Keep assumptions explicit.
7. Demo scope is safe, non-operational R&D: inspection, maintenance, sensing, logistics, reliability, monitoring.
8. The MVP goal is process orchestration, not training a new model.
